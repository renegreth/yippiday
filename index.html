What Yippiday does

🎂

Always the next day

Birthdays with age, anniversaries, holidays and trips. The widget counts down by itself – in hours on the last day.

🎨

Your own look

Over 25 templates, three colours, gradients, patterns, fonts, see-through – and save your own styles.

✨

A party on the day

Confetti, balloons, snow and animated pictures – and a morning reminder if you like.

🔒

Stays on your phone

No account, no ads, no tracking. The app does not use the internet.
