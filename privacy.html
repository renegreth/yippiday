Privacy policy

For the Android app Yippiday (package io.github.renegreth.yippiday), published on Google Play by René Greth. Last updated 20 November 2026.

In short

Yippiday does not collect, sell or share any information about you. The developer receives no data from the app. Everything you enter is stored on your phone.

Who is responsible

René Greth develops Yippiday. For any question about privacy, write to support@memonudge.com.

What the app stores – and where

  • Your days: name, date, emoji, a phone number if you type one in, and how each day looks.
  • Your settings: such as reminders, theme and widget adjustments.
  • Photos you pick for a day: a copy is kept in the app's own storage.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. The app has no internet access, no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools, so nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup (backup to your Google account, or transfer to a new phone), Android may include your days and settings in that backup so they can be restored. This backup is made and kept by Google in your own account, under Google's privacy policy; the developer cannot see it. Photos are excluded and never leave the phone this way. You can turn Android backup off in your phone's settings.

The app's own Export backup makes a file that you choose where to save or share. It holds your days and settings, but no photos.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) – only if you turn reminders on, to show a message on the morning of a day.
  • Start after restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) – so widgets and reminders keep working after the phone restarts.

Phone numbers you type in are used only for the "Call" and "Text" buttons on the day; the app hands the number to your phone's dialer or messaging app. The app does not read your contacts. Photos are picked with Android's photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you choose.

Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android's app sandbox protects from other apps. Because the app never sends data over the internet, there is no server that could be breached. Keep your phone locked and your backup files private to protect your data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept on your phone for as long as the app is installed. You are in control:

  • Delete a single day in the app (Edit a day → ⋮ → Delete). Its photo is deleted with it.
  • Delete everything: Android Settings → Apps → Yippiday → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Delete an exported backup file as you would any other file.

The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side and no account to close.

Children

Yippiday is suitable for everyone. It collects no information about anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top are updated. Changes that matter will also be mentioned in the app's "What's new".
