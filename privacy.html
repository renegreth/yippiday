Privacy policy

Applies to the Yippiday Android app. Last updated 19 November 2026.

In short

Yippiday collects no information about you. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores – and where

Your days (name, date, emoji, a phone number if you enter one, look) and your settings are stored only in the app's own storage on the phone. Photos you pick for a day are copied into the app's storage and never leave the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

No internet, account, ads or tracking

The app has no internet access. There is no account, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

The app asks for two permissions only:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) – only if you turn reminders on, for a message on the morning of the day.
  • Start after restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) – so widgets and reminders keep working after the phone restarts.

Phone numbers you type in yourself are used only for the "Call" and "Text" buttons on the day. The app does not read your contacts.

Backup

Exporting a backup makes a file that you choose where to save or share. It holds your days and settings, but no photos.

Children

The app collects no information, about children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top are updated.
